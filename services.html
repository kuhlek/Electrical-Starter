Electrical services

Safe, certified electrical work for homes and businesses.

Residential

Your home's wiring should be safe, reliable and ready for how you live. We handle everything from new circuits and lighting to distribution board upgrades and full rewires, keeping disruption low and your home clean.

  • New circuits, sockets, switches and lighting
  • Distribution board upgrades and surge protection
  • Full and partial rewires
  • LED lighting design and installation
  • Smart home, data points and CCTV cabling

Commercial

Businesses cannot afford power problems. We design, install and maintain commercial electrical systems to the required standards, scheduling work around trading hours to protect your operations.

  • Office, retail and warehouse installations
  • Energy-efficient LED lighting upgrades
  • Three-phase supply and heavy equipment connections
  • Emergency and exit lighting
  • Planned maintenance with compliance reporting

Servicing and maintenance

Flickering lights, warm sockets and tripping breakers are warnings, not nuisances. We diagnose the cause, repair it properly and tell you plainly what needs attention now and what can wait.

  • Fault finding and repair
  • Replacement of damaged sockets, switches and fittings
  • Checks of breakers, earth leakage and surge protection
  • Geyser and appliance connection repairs
  • Preventive maintenance inspections

Inspections and certificates

An Electrical Certificate of Compliance shows that an installation meets the required safety standards. Our team inspects, repairs anything that fails and issues the certificate, so your transaction is never held up.

  • Full inspection and testing of the installation
  • A written list of any defects
  • Repair of non-compliant work
  • Issue of the Certificate of Compliance
  • Pre-purchase electrical inspections

Solar and backup power

Power cuts and rising tariffs have made backup power essential. We design and install inverter, battery and solar systems sized to your needs, wired safely and certified, so you get dependable power without guesswork.

  • Needs assessment and system sizing
  • Inverter and battery installation
  • Solar panel mounting and wiring
  • Changeover switches and protection
  • Certificate of Compliance and handover training

Air conditioning

Comfort should not cost the earth. We supply, install and service split and multi-split air conditioning for homes and businesses, with dedicated circuits and clean, tidy installations.

  • Supply and install split and multi-split systems
  • Dedicated electrical circuits and isolators
  • Neat pipe, trunking and drainage routing
  • Servicing and cleaning programmes
  • Repairs and replacements

Emergency electrical

Electrical emergencies are dangerous and cannot wait. Our team responds around the clock to make your property safe, find the fault and restore power as quickly as it can be done properly.

  • Make the installation safe immediately
  • Trace and repair the fault
  • Restore power safely

  • Provide a written report for insurers
